# Build a GOAT: content and expansions proposal

From the content and expansions designer. Sources: index.html (read only, nothing edited), the playtest reports for g1a to g2c and r26a to r30c, the notes in g3a to g3c, and docs/overnight-report.md. Report codes like (r27c) point to the playtest round where a tester said it.

---

## Part 1: What content exists today, and where it feels thin

### What is in the game now

| Area | What's there |
|---|---|
| High school | 34 random season events, 18 summer choices (14 gambles, 4 safe), class rankings, recruiting stars, 3 college offers that each come with a perk |
| College | 32 random events, 20 summer choices, then the draft |
| NABL offseason | The summer decision: 6 high-risk, 8 medium and 6 safe options, plus a few that depend on age. 15 career moments, each used once per career: Trade Demand, Trade Talks, Contract Year, Bench Role, Superteam, Called Out, Mentor the Rookie, Signature Shoe, Olympics, Dunk Contest, New Coach, Tank Season, Social Media Storm, Recruit a Star, Train With a Legend. Also free agency, trade requests, Ask Out, the "your game is changing" choice, rehab, the farewell tour, and Is It Time |
| Midseason | 13 moments, each used once per career, with a 40% chance of one each season |
| Clutch final plays | 8 offensive plays, 3 "need a three" plays, 4 defensive plays. Each has 2 or 3 lines for a make and 2 or 3 for a miss |
| Story text | About 20 flavor lines on the season card. History lines cover your rival, your classmates, your protégé and climbs on the all-time lists |
| Season goals | 3 goals a season, picked from about 17 kinds |
| Eras | 6 (80s, 90s, 2000s, 2010s, 2020s, 2030s) |
| Long-term progress | 35 achievements, a Badge Book with 29 badges, the Hall of Builds (top 15 careers), a GOAT standard for each difficulty, your past GOATs listed as legends in future careers, the Hall of Fame speech, retired jerseys |
| Store | GTP Store with 12 one-time perks. Equipment: 10 items × 3 levels, and 1,700 gems maxes all of it. Gem packs from $1.99 to $49.99, Remove Ads at $3.99, and one optional ad at the end of a career that doubles its gems and GTP |
| Daily Build | Same era and same dice for everyone that day, played on Pro with no perks or gear. Your best runs are kept on your device only (top 10) |

### What players love (keep it, build on it)
- **Clutch final plays are the favorite thing in the game.** Testers call them the best part in g1a, g1c, g2c, r27c, r28c, r29c and r30c.
- **Midseason moments feel varied and quick** (r26b, r30a). One tester saw "9 different moments with no repeats" (r30b).
- **Rival stories feel alive** (r27b, r28b, r29b), and the first-MVP and first-ring banners land well (g2b).
- Every round scores fun at 4 out of 5, so the core loop works. What's missing is (a) variety in the moments that come up most often, (b) a real payoff at the big moments, and (c) reasons to come back once a career is over.

### Where it feels thin or repetitive
1. **The clutch plays repeat.** Game 6 and Game 7 used "the exact same line" (g1b, r26b). The same Conference Finals Game 7 against the same team came up two years running (r26c). One tester saw "lost Game 6, then won Game 7, same script twice in 5 seasons" (r29c), and another said "nearly every season has a Game 7 final-play prompt" (r26a). Big men get few options that suit them (g2c).
2. **Summers repeat.** "Summer options repeat a lot (guru, film coordinator, barnstorming tour)" (r26b). One tester won the streetball tournament 6 times out of 6 (r29a). "Reinvent your game" just paid points when the tester expected a new playstyle (g1b).
3. **The ending gets skipped.** The farewell season has no season card (g1b, g2b, r27c, r28c, r29c, r30c). The end-of-career highlights leave out most of the story and come out of order (r26c, r27c, r28c, r29c). The plaque is a plain card (g2a).
4. **Late career runs on autopilot** (g1b, g2b), and the offseasons stack up stops: 4 to 6 taps a season on a button called Whole Career (r26c, r27c, r28c, r30c).
5. **The people around you have no faces.** Classmate news reuses one template, with names the tester never saw in their class list (r26b, r27b, r28b). Four of five free agency offers were "word-for-word identical" (g2c). In most careers the rival never retires, so the "new rival rises" moment almost never happens (r26b, r27b, r28b, r30b). The rivalry verdict can look silly (r27c, r28b).
6. **Choices don't show their payoff.** A midseason result is "just a sentence" (r26a, r28a, r29c). Both Train With a Legend choices say "+5 pts" (r26c). This is built into the current design: every skill reward is now paid as bonus points ("+N pts"), so two options often read the same. New content has to make choices differ in **what happens** (your role, your team, the story, who you meet), not just in the number of points.
7. **Bad careers just end.** One tester was cut at 20 with 177 points banked and no warning (r27c). On Hall of Fame difficulty, "many players never stick", and a cut is a dead end.
8. **There's very little off-court life:** a shoe deal, a movie, a podcast, a magazine cover, a social media storm. No family, no hometown, no money, nothing after you retire. One tester asked for a barber or locker room, a draft-day suit and the player in team colors (g2a).
9. **There's little reason to come back.** After the plaque, the only thing to do is start the next player. The Daily Build is solo, with no sharing and no streak. Once Equipment is maxed, gems have nothing left to buy. On Pro, THE GOAT feels hopeless or unclear ("show what a GOAT-tier path looks like", g2c). Rookie players reach GOAT on their first or second career (g1a, g1b, g2b) and then have nothing new to chase.

### Ground rules every proposal below follows
- **Stop budget.** Whole Career testers already count 4 to 6 taps a season. All choices share one budget: at most 2 choice stops per offseason, 1 midseason, and 2 clutch plays per postseason. When the budget is full, a moment either doesn't happen or becomes a story line with no stat effect. Most new story is told on cards, not as stops. Story windows stay, and players can skip them.
- **Growth stays as points the player places.** No auto-assign, no hidden stats shown before the end screen, and no automatic pick that grants growth.
- **Odds stay on target.** Any new choice that moves stats or points goes through the bulk career sim (autosim), so GOAT odds stay at: Rookie about 9 to 10%; Pro about 1% casual, 4.3 to 5% smart; Hall of Fame about 0.5% casual, 2 to 2.5% smart. Text-only changes need no check.
- **Rookie earns nothing**: no GTP, gems, achievements, collection rewards or challenge medals.
- **Original names only, and the NABL on screen.** The game already swaps "NBA" to "NABL" when it draws text.
- **Paid content is cosmetics or new ways to play.** It never adds power beyond today's Equipment, and it never locks the core career.

## Part 2: Ranked proposals

### 1. Clutch Moments 2.0: more situations, more lines, never the same script twice
- **What it is:** More kinds of final play. You're at the line for two, down 1. An inbound with 0.8 seconds left (catch-and-shoot or a lob only). Up 2 and fouled, so you have to ice the free throws. An overtime final play. A road Game 7 in front of a hostile crowd. Big-man options in every situation: tip-in, lob, seal and score, kick out and crash (g2c). Each play gets 5 or 6 result lines instead of 2 or 3, and no line repeats within a career. Lines name the opponent, the city and your age ("At 36, the old man still has it"). Game 6 and Game 7 never share a score or a line. An injured player is never handed the last shot (r27c). Finally, a cap of 2 clutch stops per postseason. Other close games are still played by the normal sim and show up as a highlight line ("You hit the go-ahead free throws with 9 seconds left"), so the cap changes nothing about who wins.
- **Player fantasy:** Every big shot is its own story, and you remember it.
- **How it fits:** Add to the CL_OFF, CL_DEF and CL_THREE lists, and add new situation types inside clutchPend(). Line memory works the same way flavor() already avoids repeats, and highlights() picks up the new lines automatically.
- **Effort:** Small for the lines and the no-repeat memory; medium for the new situation types.
- **Risk:** New situations need starting odds tuned so the overall playoff win rate doesn't move. Check with the bulk sim. The text-only half carries no risk.

### 2. The Final Chapter: farewell season, last game, jersey night, epilogue
- **What it is:**
  - The farewell season gets its own season card, which is skipped today (6 reports). Each road city gives you a gift line (a rocking chair, a painting, your old high school band plays).
  - Your last home game asks one question, "How do you go out?": take the last shot, sub out to a standing ovation, or hand the ball to your protégé. It's story only.
  - Each team that retires your jersey gets its own banner moment (built on the existing retired-jerseys list).
  - The Hall of Fame speech gains a choice of presenter: your rival, your high school coach, your protégé or a family member.
  - A one-tap "Where are they now" epilogue lets you pick a life after basketball: coach, broadcaster, front office, business owner, foundation, or disappear to a ranch. It writes 2 or 3 lines onto the plaque and into your Hall of Builds entry.
  - The career highlights list runs in order and includes midseason moments, trades and rival knockouts (r27c, r28c, r29c).
- **Player fantasy:** The ending plays like the last ten minutes of a sports documentary.
- **How it fits:** farewellPend() and S.farewell, finish(), endHTML(), inductionHTML(), jerseys(), highlights(). The Hall of Builds entry gets one new field for the epilogue. The epilogue picks lay the groundwork for Dynasty (#5) and Coaching (#15).
- **Effort:** Small to medium.
- **Risk:** None to balance, because nothing after the last season affects stats. It adds one extra stop per career (the last game), and it can be skipped.

### 3. Daily Build 2.0: a daily twist, a streak, a share card
- **What it is:**
  - **Daily Twist:** one shared rule for the day, from a list of about 30. Examples: "Everyone is 6-2 or shorter", "The 90s, and threes are worth less", "Every summer offers only gambles", "Your Durability is locked at 1", "Start at a mid-major".
  - **Streak:** counts the days you play in a row. Milestones (3, 7, 30 days) give cosmetics only, such as a plaque frame or a jersey trim.
  - **Share card:** a short text summary sent through the phone's share sheet, like a Wordle result: "Build a GOAT Daily · Nov 8 · Hall of Famer · GOAT 112 · 🏆🏆⭐⭐⭐⭐ · beat it?"
  - **Later:** a worldwide leaderboard through Apple's Game Center, which needs no server of your own.
- **Player fantasy:** A daily ritual with friends: "Did you get past 100 today?"
- **How it fits:** toggleDaily(), seedFrom(todayKey()) and dailyBest() already exist. The plaque already says "Screenshot the plaque and send it". A twist is just a starting condition, applied the same way the Daily already forces Pro and no perks.
- **Effort:** Small for twists, streak and share card; medium for Game Center.
- **Risk:** A twist must be a limit (a height cap, a locked trait, an era), never free stats ("premades fill skills only"), and it can't set where you're drafted ("the draft is a pure draft"). Daily rewards stay cosmetic, and Equipment stays off.

### 4. Scenario Challenges, plus a Weekly Challenge
- **What it is:** A Challenges menu of hand-made starting setups, each with a goal and a medal. All names are original. Examples:
  - **The Late Bloomer:** 6-0 and 40 fewer skill points. Win an MVP.
  - **Glass Body:** Durability locked at 1. Play 15 seasons.
  - **90s Enforcer:** 7-0 or taller in the 90s. Win 3 Defensive Player of the Year awards.
  - **Small-Market Savior:** Win a title with the team that drafted you, and never leave.
  - **Mid-Major to the Rafters:** Go to a mid-major school and get your jersey retired.
  - **Ring Chaser:** Win titles with 3 different teams.
  - **The Comeback:** Recover from a major injury before 25, then make an All-NABL team.
  - **The Specialist:** Never average 12 points, and still make the Hall of Fame.

  One challenge rotates each week with the same dice for everyone, built on the Daily's plumbing.
- **Player fantasy:** "Can I pull off the impossible version?" It gives Rookie players who reach GOAT in their first career a new goal.
- **How it fits:** Starting conditions work the way the Daily's do (difficulty, era, height range, locked traits, school level). Goals are checked in finish(), the same way achievements are. Several existing achievements are nearly challenges already: Prove Them Wrong, Little Big Man, Mid-Major Miracle, One-Team Legend, Old School.
- **Effort:** Medium: the menu, 10 to 15 scenarios, and a Challenge Book.
- **Risk:** Setups only limit you; they never give stats, and the draft stays pure. GTP rewards are small so the store doesn't flood. Rookie earns no medals.

### 5. Dynasty Mode: the next generation
- **What it is:** When a career ends, you can choose "Continue the family". Your next player is your son, entering the league about 20 years later:
  - He has your last name, and your career runs through his story: "Your father's #23 hangs in the rafters in Phoenix."
  - Legacy pressure: the media and the season goals compare him to his father ("Not his father yet").
  - Your old rival's son can turn up in his class, so the rivalry carries across generations.
  - The era moves forward one step (80s, 2000s, 2020s, 2030s and on).
  - The parent stays on the all-time list as a legend. Today only your GOATs do.
  - A Family Tree screen shows each generation, their rings and their plaques, with a family record ("The Vance family: 3 generations, 9 rings").
- **Player fantasy:** Building a basketball family dynasty, like the real father-and-son stories.
- **How it fits:** immortalize() and makeLegends() already put your past GOATs on future all-time lists, so extend that to every career in a dynasty. The rival is chosen at the draft from your class, and that rule can pick "your father's rival's son". The era order already exists. The Hall of Builds gets a family column.
- **Effort:** Medium.
- **Monetization:** A clean one-time expansion ("Dynasty Pack", about $2.99 to $4.99), or free with a paid family crest.
- **Risk:** Family is story only. No stats pass down: hidden potential is rolled fresh and stays hidden, and perks stay one-time. Hype feeds draft buzz, so give no legacy hype bonus at all, which keeps the draft pure. Check odds with the bulk sim.

### 6. Rivalry 2.0: nemesis arcs that always pay off
- **What it is:**
  - The rival shows up earlier, as a top name in your high school class rankings with one high school or college meeting. Draft night adds "He went 3 picks ahead of you."
  - Story beats arrive at set points in the career instead of at random: first playoff meeting, being called out, the team-up offer, the twilight years, the retirement handshake.
  - The rival retires on a schedule (around 34 to 36), and a "Young Gun" rises who calls you old. That gives the late career a purpose (g1b, g2b) and makes sure the handoff happens (r26b, r27b, r28b, r30b).
  - Rival types beyond the player from your class: the **Franchise Nemesis** (the team that keeps knocking you out) and the **Former Teammate** (the player who took your spot after a trade).
  - A ledger card tracks head to head, playoff series and best duels. The final verdict counts rings first (r28b).
- **Player fantasy:** A league-defining rivalry the game writes around you.
- **How it fits:** S.rival, the rival pick at the draft, the rival-retirement code at the end of each season, the Rivalry Night midseason moment, the Called Out career moment, rival clutch lines, rivalryCard(), and the rivalry verdict in endHTML().
- **Effort:** Medium.
- **Risk:** Low. Don't add new team-up power: Rookie already gets easy once you team up with your rival (g1a).

### 7. Midseason and summer refresh: more moments, by career stage, with a visible stake
- **What it is:**
  - About 12 new midseason moments, split by career stage so 22 feels different from 34:
    - **Early years:** Rookie Wall, veteran hazing, Summer League star.
    - **Prime:** All-Star captain, hometown return game, a ref says you flop.
    - **Veteran:** the Young Gun calls you old, a ring-chase buyout offer, chasing a milestone this month ("you pass a legend on the scoring list").
  - **A visible stake:** each moment states a small goal ("Win 8 of the next 12", "Score 30 in the rematch"), and the season card shows whether you hit it and the bonus points it paid (fixes r26a, r28a, r29c).
  - **Summers:** more options, set up so the two choices differ in what happens, not just points. "Reinvent your game" actually offers a new style through the existing "your game is changing" choice (g1b). No option appears 6 summers in a row (r29a).
  - Remove the duplicate Mentor the Rookie, which shows up both midseason and in the summer (r29a, r29b).
- **Player fantasy:** Every season has its own headline.
- **How it fits:** Add new entries in midPend(). The small goal reuses the season goals system (makeGoals and goalOk). summerNBA() lists, and the "already seen" memory in summerSet().
- **Effort:** Small to medium. It's all writing inside systems that already exist.
- **Risk:** More bonus points per season means more growth, which moves GOAT odds. Keep the stake rewards inside today's goal reward sizes, and check with the bulk sim.

### 8. The Trophy Room: moments you collect across careers
- **What it is:** A scrapbook that lasts across careers. Each big moment becomes a card showing your player in that team's colors, the era, and the line of text from the moment: first ring, MVP, a Finals game-winner, a 50-point night, a jersey retirement, Olympic gold, a Dunk Contest win, knocking out your rival. Collection sets give long-term goals: a ring in every era (6), a title with 10 different teams, a game-winner against every team, every midseason moment answered both ways. Finishing a set unlocks cosmetics.
- **Player fantasy:** "Look at my collection."
- **How it fits:** highlights() plus each season's playoff path and clutch records feed the cards. The Badge Book's counting pattern handles sets, and one new save key is added to the save list (the iPhone save copy already covers that list).
- **Effort:** Medium.
- **Risk:** Only Pro and Hall of Fame careers count (Rookie earns nothing). Limit the cards stored per type to keep saves small.

### 9. Locker Room cosmetics: the respectful thing to spend gems on
- **What it is:** Things to earn or buy with gems that never touch stats:
  - **Plaque frames by tier,** from bronze for a role player to an animated gold frame for THE GOAT. Frames are earned, with extra styles to buy.
  - **Draft night:** a suit and the team's cap, plus your player in team colors (g2a).
  - **Signature shoe:** you design your colorway when you sign the shoe deal, and it shows on your player.
  - **Celebration style** after a game-winner.
  - **A barber visit mid-career** to change hair and beard (g2a).
  - **Court themes** behind the season card.
- **Player fantasy:** Style, and a plaque worth screenshotting.
- **How it fits:** avatar(), the look options, teamLook(), plaqueHTML(), and the gem shop. Today gems only buy Equipment, and everything maxes at 1,700 gems, so players who spend the most run out of things to buy.
- **Effort:** Medium. Most of the cost is art.
- **Risk:** None to balance. This overlaps the art and UI designer's work, so coordinate. Keep earnable versions so free players get some too.

### 10. A named supporting cast: your co-star, your coach, your running mate
- **What it is:** Each team you join shows a named co-star (a real player already in the league sim), a head coach with a style ("defense first", "pace and space", "old school"), and a veteran. Moments call them by name: "Your co-star Andre Kale goes down", "Coach Ruiz is fired after a 25-win season", "Your running mate of 6 seasons retires". Free agency offers describe each team's players, coach and title chances, so they stop being "word-for-word identical" (g2c).
- **Player fantasy:** The team feels like a team, not a number.
- **How it fits:** The team's roster in the league sim, the Co-Star Goes Down and New Head Coach moments, and the offer text in faPend(). A coach is just a named person per team, with a style tag that decides which moments appear.
- **Effort:** Medium.
- **Risk:** Keep coach style as flavor plus moment choice. If it changes team strength, it moves odds.

### 11. Second Chance: the road back from overseas or the minor league
- **What it is:** Getting cut or going undrafted no longer ends the career. You get an offer from an overseas club in an original-name league (for example the "Adriatic Pro League") or from the Minor League (the game already shows the G League as "Minor League"). You play 1 or 2 quick seasons there, with a short story and one decision a season. A big season earns an NABL tryout or a 10-day contract: "The kid who got cut at 20 is back at 23." Before a cut, the game warns you if you have points banked (r27c).
- **Player fantasy:** The underdog comeback. Hall of Fame difficulty becomes a fight instead of a dead end.
- **How it fits:** The "Out of the league" exit in the season-end code, the summer choice system for the overseas offseasons, and the existing "Prove Them Wrong" achievement.
- **Effort:** Medium to big. The overseas seasons need a simple results sim.
- **Risk:** Longer careers for weak players nudge GOAT odds up a little, though probably very little, since cut players rarely become stars. Check with the bulk sim.

### 12. Off-court life: hometown, reputation and money
- **What it is:** Three light story threads that change which moments show up. None of them adds a stat.
  - **Hometown:** a homecoming game, your hometown team in free agency ("Come home?"), and building a court in your old neighborhood.
  - **Reputation:** choices you already make (fire back, clap back, demand a trade versus stay loyal, mentor, log off) move you between Fan Favorite and Villain. Villains get booing road crowds in their clutch lines; fan favorites get loyalty stories. The plaque ends with a line like "The league's favorite villain."
  - **Money and brand:** the shoe line, a business, a foundation. One decision every few seasons, summed up on an end-screen Life card. Net worth is flavor, not a stat.
- **Player fantasy:** The life around the game.
- **How it fits:** The career decision log, how often moments in eventPend() and midPend() come up, a home-team option in faPend(), and a new card in endHTML().
- **Effort:** Medium.
- **Risk:** Reputation is shown on screen, so it isn't a hidden stat. Its effects stay limited to which story appears.

### 13. Era Packs: new eras with their own stories
- **What it is:** New eras on top of today's 6:
  - **The Golden Age:** set shots, giant centers, train travel.
  - **The 70s:** high-flyers in a rival league (original name) that merges into the NABL mid-career. The merger is itself a story event.
  - **The 2040s:** a 4-point line and players tracked by sensors.

  Each era brings its own summer options, flavor lines, clutch lines and one era achievement.
- **Player fantasy:** "What would my player look like in that league?"
- **How it fits:** The era list already holds each era's style, pace and three-point rate. The legends' era labels, the era order, and the summer filter (which already hides NIL deals and podcasts in older eras) carry over.
- **Effort:** Medium per era: writing plus a balance check.
- **Monetization:** One-time packs of two eras each, or unlocked through achievements. This is new places to play, not power.
- **Risk:** Each era changes which builds pay off, so run the bulk sim per era. The league merger needs care so the team list stays consistent.

### 14. Legends Mode: replay or face your own legends
- **What it is:**
  - **Era Swap:** load a saved build's skills into a new career in a different era: "Would my 80s GOAT survive the 2020s?" You still start in high school, and hidden potential is rolled fresh.
  - **Your Legend Returns:** for one career, your old GOAT is an active star in the league as your rival, or he anchors the team you have to beat.
- **Player fantasy:** "My guy against my other guy."
- **How it fits:** The Hall of Builds stores only results today (top 15), so finish() would also need to save the skills. makeLegends() and the rival pick handle "Returns".
- **Effort:** Medium to big.
- **Risk:** It loads skills only (like premades), never traits or perks. Perks still cost GTP.

### 15. Coaching career after retirement (big expansion)
- **What it is:** If you pick "Coach" in the epilogue (#2), a separate short mode lets you coach an NABL team for 5 to 10 quick seasons. Each season you pick a system, choose a star to feature, make one draft pick from the league's real draft class, and take one trade call. Your old teammates and your protégé turn up. Coaching rings and Coach of the Year awards go on the family plaque.
- **Player fantasy:** "I was the GOAT. Can I build one?"
- **How it fits:** The league sim already plays every team every season. Your choices nudge your team's strength. It needs a new set of screens.
- **Effort:** Big.
- **Monetization:** A natural paid expansion.
- **Risk:** It's its own mode, so it doesn't revive the tabled hands-off Whole Career mode, and it has no effect on player GOAT odds.

## Part 3: Suggested order and how it pays

- **Free update 1, "Moments" (mostly writing):** #1 Clutch 2.0, #2 Final Chapter, #7 Midseason and summer refresh, #3 Daily 2.0. These fix the most-cited thin spots and add a daily reason to open the game.
- **Free update 2, "The League Around You":** #6 Rivalry 2.0, #10 Supporting cast, #4 Challenges with the Weekly Challenge, #8 Trophy Room.
- **Gem sink, any time:** #9 Locker Room cosmetics. It gives gems a use after Equipment is maxed, without touching balance.
- **Paid one-time expansions (about $2.99 to $4.99, like Remove Ads):** #5 Dynasty Pack, #13 Era Packs, #15 Coaching, and later #14 Legends Mode. #11 Second Chance and #12 Off-court life work as free depth or as part of the Dynasty Pack.
- **Do not sell:** GTP, clutch retries, summer re-rolls, or anything that changes odds. They would break "the game must not be easy" and make THE GOAT less rare. Keep the single optional ad, and add no new ads that change outcomes.

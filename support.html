Common questions

I bought something but didn't get it.
Open the Gem Shop and tap Restore Purchases. Remove Ads and the New Player Special come back on any device signed in to the same Apple ID. If gems are missing, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is my career saved?
Your careers are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

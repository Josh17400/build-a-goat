Privacy Policy

Build a GOAT · Last updated November 15, 2026

Build a GOAT is a single-player basketball career game. This page explains what information the app uses and why.

No accounts, no personal details

You do not create an account, and the app never asks for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos. Your saved careers, gems and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, basic device information, and how you interact with ads, in order to show and measure ads.

  • On iPhone and iPad, the app asks your permission before it can use your advertising identifier for tracking (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt). If you say no, you still get ads, but they are not personalized.
  • Buying "Remove Ads" or the New Player Special turns banner ads off. Optional rewarded videos (watched only if you choose to) stay available.
  • Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

In-app purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Purchases are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore them. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for this app. It does not receive your name, email or payment details.

RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all game data stored on your device. To ask about data held by our ad or purchase partners, contact us below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
